Ark — icon concepts

Each shown at 168px, then at Dock scale (64 / 32) on dark and light.

Ark
The current hull on an Arc-style tile. Cabin dropped — it was the first thing to close up at 32pt.
Covenant
Genesis 9's arc: the sign after the ark, and literally an arc. Dark tile, colour carried by the mark.
Aperture
No boat at all. One open ring — Arc's move, which is pure geometry on a gradient.
Swell
The hull dissolved into the water it rides: one ribbon, one wake.
Keel
Same mark as Ark, tile and mark inverted. Reads bright in a dark Dock rather than dark in a light one.
